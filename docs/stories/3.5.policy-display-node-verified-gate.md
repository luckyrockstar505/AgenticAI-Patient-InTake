## Story

**As a** verified member,  
**I want** a clear summary of my coverage,  
**so that** I understand what my plan covers before I file.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Asserts `state.verified is True` before calling `get_policy_view`; otherwise raises `VerificationInvariantError` (caught → handoff).
2. Stores `policy_view` in state; renders a concise summary (plan, dates, deductible/OOP remaining, key copays) plus a structured `policy_card` payload for the UI.
3. Ends with a prompt: "Would you like to file a claim?"
4. `ask_question` about coverage in later phases answers from `policy_view` only (no hallucinated benefits); unknown → says so.
5. Terminated coverage → shows status and still allows claim for dates within the prior coverage period.
