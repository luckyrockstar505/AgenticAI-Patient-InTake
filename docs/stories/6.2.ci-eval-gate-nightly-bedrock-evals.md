## Story

**As a** team,  
**I want** evals enforced on every PR and a nightly run against the real model,  
**so that** regressions are caught before merge and model drift is visible.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `ci.yml` gains job `eval-mock` (required check) running `make eval`.
2. `.github/workflows/nightly-eval.yml` runs `make eval-live` with OIDC-assumed AWS role; posts summary to job summary; uploads results artifact.
3. Thresholds read from `evals/thresholds.yaml`; safety scorers are hard gates (must be 100%).
